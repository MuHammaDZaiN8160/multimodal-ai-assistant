General Billing Policies and Charge Explanations

STATEMENT BALANCE TERMS
A credit card statement balance is the total amount owed at the close of a billing cycle. It includes all new charges, any previous unpaid balance, interest, and fees minus any payments or credits applied during the period.

PREVIOUS BALANCE
The Previous Balance shown on a statement is the amount carried over from the last billing period. It is the closing balance from your prior statement.

NEW CHARGES
New charges are all transactions posted to your account during the current billing cycle, including purchases, cash advances, and balance transfers.

PAYMENTS AND CREDITS
Payments Received represents money you have paid toward your balance during the billing cycle. Credits may include refunds, disputed charge reversals, or promotional credits.

TAXES AND FEES
Taxes & Fees line items typically include government-mandated taxes on certain services and account maintenance fees such as annual fees, late payment fees, or foreign transaction fees.

MINIMUM PAYMENT
The Minimum Payment Due is the smallest amount you must pay by the due date to keep the account in good standing. Paying only the minimum will result in interest charges on the remaining balance.

INTEREST CHARGES
If you carry a balance from month to month, interest is charged on the unpaid portion at the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) disclosed in your card agreement.

DISPUTED CHARGES
If you do not recognize a charge, you have the right to dispute it within 60 days of the statement date. Contact your card issuer's customer service immediately.

BILLING CYCLE
A billing cycle is typically 28–31 days. All transactions made within a cycle appear on the same statement.
